## How onboarding works

Get from zero to a live, traffic-routing gateway in three steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a gateway">
    Provision a new gateway instance through the dashboard or the REST API. Each gateway gets a unique endpoint URL that your consumers will call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a route">
    Define a route by mapping an inbound path and HTTP method to an upstream URL. Apply plugins — authentication, rate limiting, transformations — directly on the route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first request">
    Send a real HTTP request through your gateway endpoint and watch it proxy to your upstream service. Confirm success in the analytics dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
